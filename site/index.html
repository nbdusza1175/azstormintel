What it does

Storm intensity

NOAA MRMS radar rainfall in 5-minute steps over each basin, compared to NOAA Atlas 14 to give a return period.

Flood intensity

Peak discharge at the basin outlet from terrain, soils, land cover and soil moisture, compared to USGS flood-frequency estimates.

Any storm, back 10 years

Pick a time window and the analysis runs on public data: live radar, recent events, or storms from past seasons.

Gauges as checks

Rain and stream gauges are shown alongside the results to check them, never to tune them, so the same inputs give the same answer.
